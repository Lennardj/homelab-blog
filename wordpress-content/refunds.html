Cancellations and refunds

  • More than one week before the camp starts — full refund, no questions asked.
  • Within one week of the start date — no refund, because the place has been held and cannot usually be filled at short notice. If there is a waiting list and your place is taken by someone else, I will refund you in full.

To cancel, email holidaycamp@lennardjohn.org with the student's name and which session you booked. Refunds go back to the card used for the booking and usually take five to ten working days to appear.

If I cancel

If a session is cancelled for any reason — too few bookings, illness, venue problems — you get a full refund, whenever that happens. Where possible I will offer a place in another session first.

Changing sessions

If you need to move between the morning and afternoon session, email me. I will move you if there is room, at no charge.

Missed days

The camp runs as a five-day programme where each day builds on the one before. Refunds are not given for individual days missed, but tell me if a student will be away and I will help them catch up.

Your rights

Nothing here limits your rights under the New Zealand Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 or the Fair Trading Act 1986.
