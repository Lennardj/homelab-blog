Holiday tech camps for kids.

Hands-on sessions during the school holidays where young people build something real rather than watching a demonstration. Small classes, so every child gets attention.

Classes

Places are limited per class. The bar shows how many are left — when a class fills, booking closes and you can email me to go on the list for a cancellation.

[lj_camp_classes]

What to expect

  • Small classes with a hard cap, so nobody gets lost at the back.
  • Building real things — not slideshows.
  • All equipment provided.

Booking

Booking is per child, per class. You will be asked for the child's name, age and an emergency contact at checkout.

Any medical or dietary information is collected separately closer to the camp date, rather than stored online.
